Selected work/Labcorp

Healthcare communications

Making complex clinical information clear, human, and actionable.

Clinical communications have to do two things at once: honor the complexity of the subject and make it understandable to the people who need it. For Labcorp, LMC brought those demands together through clear creative direction, video, and animation.

Services
  • Creative direction
  • Video production
  • Animation
  • Visual storytelling

Overview

Clarity is part of the work.

Healthcare audiences are asked to absorb unfamiliar information, evaluate its relevance, and decide what to do next. The creative cannot simply look polished. It has to reduce friction, establish trust, and help the message land without oversimplifying it.

Selected Labcorp Patient Access film

The challenge

Translate expertise without losing the audience.

Scientific and clinical material can become dense quickly. The challenge was to create communications that felt credible to knowledgeable stakeholders while remaining direct and approachable for a broader audience.

The response

Build a visual language around understanding.

LMC used creative direction, live-action video, and animation as complementary tools. Information was organized into a clear hierarchy; motion helped explain rather than decorate; and the overall tone stayed precise, contemporary, and human.

The result

A flexible system for important information.

The work established a consistent way to communicate complex subjects across formats. The result is content that supports the Labcorp brand while giving each message the structure and breathing room it needs.
