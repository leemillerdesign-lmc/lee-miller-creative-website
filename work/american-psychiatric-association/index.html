Selected work/American Psychiatric Association

Membership communications

A member story built around the people who know the profession best.

The American Psychiatric Association wanted to engage an important member segment with a film that felt focused, relevant, and authentic. LMC helped shape the story from script feedback through interviews and final delivery.

Services
  • Creative direction
  • Script feedback
  • Interview direction
  • Video production

Overview

Professional audiences recognize what is real.

Membership communications can easily feel institutional. The opportunity was to make the message personal by grounding it in the experience and language of the people the organization exists to serve.

Selected film

Selected American Psychiatric Association membership film

The challenge

Connect with a specific member audience.

The film needed to communicate the value of belonging without becoming a list of benefits. It also had to balance multiple perspectives, stay focused on the core message, and move smoothly from planning into production.

The response

Let meaningful interviews carry the story.

LMC provided feedback on the script, guided the interview process, and shaped the final film around direct, credible voices. The production stayed disciplined and on schedule while leaving room for the unexpected moments that make an interview feel human.

The result

A compelling film with a clear sense of audience.

The final piece gave the APA a focused membership story that felt informed by the profession itself. The collaboration kept the message, schedule, and creative decisions moving together.
